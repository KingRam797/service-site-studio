# Sellable Offer

The customer is buying a clearer path from interest to action—not a pile of pages.

These are launch prices for validating the productized service. Review them after three paid deliveries using actual delivery time, revisions, and customer response.

## Proof Page — from $499

For a service business with one clear offer and a manual contact process.

Includes:

- one distinctive, mobile-first page;
- positioning, service menu, proof/gallery, about, FAQ/policies, and contact action;
- supplied logo, approved photography, and social links;
- email or SMS inquiry routing;
- essential metadata and structured business information;
- deployment and owner handoff;
- one structured revision round.

Target delivery: 5–7 business days after complete intake and assets.

Does not include custom booking logic, a database, payment processing, copy-heavy migrations, ecommerce, or ongoing content work.

## Booking Ready — from $999

For appointments, consultations, sessions, and quote-based services.

Includes everything in Proof Page, plus:

- custom intake or booking-request flow;
- external booking-provider connection or defined request workflow;
- service duration, availability, policy, and confirmation logic;
- richer proof and conversion tracking;
- two structured revision rounds.

Target delivery: 7–10 business days after complete intake, assets, and third-party access.

Internal availability calculations, deposits, and database-backed holds move to Operations Site.

## Operations Site — from $1,799

For businesses whose website must enforce how work is scheduled, paid for, or fulfilled.

Includes everything in Booking Ready, plus a scoped combination of:

- deposit or payment flow;
- database-backed requests or reservations;
- capacity, overlap, buffer, fulfillment-window, or order-size rules;
- transactional owner notifications;
- integration and recovery documentation;
- three structured revision rounds.

Target delivery: 10–15 business days after complete intake, assets, business-rule approval, and provider access.

Final price depends on the number of operational modules and third-party systems—not page count alone.

## Payment structure

- 50% to reserve the build window.
- 25% after the approved design direction and working primary page.
- 25% before production launch and transfer.
- Third-party subscriptions, domains, transaction fees, licensed assets, and rush work are client costs unless explicitly included.

## Change control

A revision changes an approved implementation within scope. A new service line, new integration, changed business model, new audience, or replaced visual direction is a scope change and receives a written estimate.

## Marketplace positioning

**Headline:** I will build a distinctive service-business website with booking-ready conversion

**Short promise:** Your real work, services, policies, and customer path become one polished mobile-first site—built to launch, not linger in drafts.

**Qualification line:** Best for independent service businesses that can provide one decision-maker, clear services, approved assets, and timely feedback.

## Pricing evidence

The initial prices deliberately sit inside current marketplace ranges while leaving room to raise them after proof. [Upwork's 2026 guide](https://www.upwork.com/resources/how-much-does-it-cost-to-build-website) places many websites between $300 and $5,000, with custom small-business work commonly higher as complexity rises. [Fiverr's small-business guidance](https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/business/cost-to-build-website) similarly spans roughly $500–$5,000, with commerce and integrations costing more. The package floor should be reviewed from Victor's real effective hourly return, not copied from marketplace averages.
